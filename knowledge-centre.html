Operational Curriculum

Six Pillars Of Arcade Commercial Literacy

Everything required to transition from a curious beginner to a disciplined multi-machine operator.

1. The Amusement Concept & Cash Flow

Understand the psychology of instant tangible reward. Learn why amusement machines generate higher gross yield per square foot than traditional retail, with zero inventory spoilage.

  • 50-30-20 cash flow allocation rule.
  • Why physical prize games outlast digital fads.

2. How To Select The Right Machine

Match game mechanics to venue demographics: Claw machines for cafes, high-speed 10 on 10 timers for sweet shops, and Cut A Prize showcases for prime shopping mall atriums.

  • Footprint calculations (7 to 10 sq ft).
  • Claw tension settings vs win ratio.

3. Venue Negotiation & Placement

How to approach hotel general managers, sweet shop owners, and cafe proprietors. We provide battle-tested pitch decks, agreement drafts, and revenue-sharing contracts.

  • Space rent vs revenue share negotiation.
  • Power consumption & electrical safety.

4. Toy & Prize Inventory Management

Master the art of visual merchandising inside the glass cabinet. Learn how Plush O Soft size tiers (Below 12cm, 20cm, 30cm) influence player repeat rate and cost of goods sold.

  • 10-minute weekly refilling routines.
  • Top-shelf showstopper placement tactics.

5. Upkeep, Servicing & Toolkits

Understand modular hot-swappable architecture. How to use your Master Technician Toolkit to diagnose optical sensors, test coin acceptors, and clean gantry guide rails.

  • The 3-minute PCB swap protocol.
  • Anti-fishing optical gate calibration.

6. Cashless QR Payments & Telemetry

Utilize SCANO Smart Displays to eliminate coin collection runs. Setup direct UPI QR bank deposits, track live play counts, and review automated revenue splits.

  • Cashless payment reconciliation.
  • Multi-machine remote telemetry monitoring.
