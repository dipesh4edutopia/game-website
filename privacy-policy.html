Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At Game & Games Amusement ("Company", "we", "us", or "our"), respecting the privacy and data rights of our business partners, arcade operators, host venues, and customers is foundational to our philosophy. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding the collection, use, and protection of information submitted across our website and telematics interfaces.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Request a business callback, quotation, or equipment consultation.
  • Inquire about franchise or EMI financing models.
  • Submit a career application or apprentice inquiry.
  • Interact with our browser-based shopping cart and checkout demonstration.

This information may include your full name, telephone number, business email, host venue commercial address, and equipment preferences.

2. Use of Information

Information gathered is strictly utilized to:

  • Provide personalized equipment quotations and location compatibility feedback.
  • Coordinate telephone consultations regarding machine delivery, warranty, and technical toolkits.
  • Dispatch customer-requested publications such as the Handbook of Wisdom.
  • Enhance website performance, browser navigation, and customer support responsiveness.

3. Local Storage & Cookies

Our Phase 1 static website uses client-side localStorage solely to preserve your shopping cart items, size variant selections, and badge counts across page reloads. No invasive advertising tracking cookies or unauthorized third-party trackers are utilized.

4. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We never sell, trade, or rent operator contact details, host venue agreements, or machine placement addresses to outside marketing aggregators. All commercial inquiries remain strictly confidential.

5. Contacting Our Data Privacy Team

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to modify any previously submitted contact details, please reach out to:
