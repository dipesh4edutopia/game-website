Two Decades Of Excellence

20+ Years Of Experience. Finally Revealed.

The story behind Game & Games Amusement. From humble engineering beginnings to pioneering India's smartest, most reliable arcade ecosystem.

"You Are The One That Inspire Us To Always Stay Ahead Of The Curve."

For more than 20 years, our team has operated on the frontlines of the amusement and coin-operated gaming industry. We observed how traditional arcade operators struggled: unreliable foreign machines, fragile circuit boards that took months to replace, lack of authentic spare parts, and poor-quality prizes that failed to excite customers.

We founded Game & Games Amusement with an uncompromising objective: build machines that earn more, repair less, grow faster, and keep operators stress-free. Today, we are proud to be the first in India to introduce Smart Display & multi-game QR technology, bridging classic arcade joy with seamless digital UPI payments.

Our Core Philosophy

The Three Pillars That Define Everything We Build

1. WHO WE ARE

We are machine architects, electro-mechanical technicians, and commercial mentors with deep domain expertise in the Indian amusement and entertainment sector.

  • Parent Company: G&G Amusement.
  • Technology Division: SCANO (Play Smart).
  • Quality Standards: Commercial-grade steel and BIS safety compliant.

2. WHAT WE DO

We engineer turnkey arcade hardware, supply high-appeal prize inventory, provide genuine OEM spare parts, and structure risk-mitigated business opportunities.

  • 5 Proven Game Formats (Claw, Clip, Timer, Cut, Stacker).
  • Curated plush assortments in three calibrated sizes.
  • Master technician toolkits and 24-hour spare parts dispatch.

3. WHY WE DO IT

Because traditional businesses often trap entrepreneurs with crushing rents, high wages, and perishable waste. Amusement games provide a high-yield, resilient alternative.

  • Zero perishable inventory risk.
  • Compact 7 to 10 sq ft commercial footprint.
  • True passive income with autonomous operation.
Engineering Discipline

Built Like High-Volume Commercial Infrastructure

Just as renowned high-volume food brands like Rameshwaram Cafe maintain rigorous operational consistency through modular equipment, we design our arcade cabinets for maximum uptime and quick serviceability.

  • Hot-Swappable Components: Power supplies, coin mechs, and PCBs swap out in 3 minutes via quick-connect terminal clips.
  • Rigorous 72-Hour Burn-In: Every machine is tested for 72 continuous operating hours prior to dispatch.
  • Anti-Cheat Optical Protection: Prevents fraudulent wire fishing and mechanical cabinet tilting.
