Specialized Brand Divisions Powering Indian Amusement

As detailed in our original ecosystem architecture (Reference Image 2), we operate five focused brand pillars under one umbrella: manufacturing, telematics, prize merchandising, advertising monetization, and operator education.

Parent Brand • Established 20+ Years

G&G Amusement

"Give It A Try!"

The foundational manufacturing and operations backbone. Designs, fabricates, and supports high-yield arcade equipment including Pick A Prize, Press A Prize, 10 on 10, Cut A Prize, and Perfect Tower. Oversees national distribution, operator warranties, and regional franchise networks.

Turnkey Machine Manufacturing EMI & Revenue Share Structuring 1-Year Comprehensive Warranty Pan-India Logistics
Technology & Telematics Sub-Brand

SCANO

"Play Smart" • QR & Telemetry Pioneers

India's first smart display and multi-game QR technology platform specifically created for arcade cabinets. Eliminates the friction of physical coin handling by enabling direct UPI mobile payments on the machine, while providing operators real-time cloud reporting and audit meters.

Smart Cabinet Display Integration Instant UPI QR Payment Gateway Automated Cashless Settlement Remote Play Count Telemetry
Toys & Merchandising Sub-Brand

Plush O Soft

"Worth Showing Off"

Specialized prize merchandise division dedicated to creating plush toys and novelty rewards with high tactile appeal and calibrated grip dynamics. Categorized across Indian, Imported, Handmade, and Gifts lines in Below 12cm, 20cm, and 30cm size tiers.

Claw-Optimized Plush Geometry BIS Certified Child Safety Wholesale Volume Tier Pricing Custom Corporate Character Plush

ADS No Skip

"Where Every View Counts"

Monetizes captive dwell-time at amusement locations. Turns arcade cabinets into high-impact experiential branding platforms through 360-degree machine vinyl wraps, top-screen video commercial loops, physical sample product distribution, and QR discount voucher delivery.

360° Full Machine Vinyl Wraps Top Display Video Commercials Promotional Sample Product Dispensing Coupon & Voucher Distribution
