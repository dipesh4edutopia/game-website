Operational Reliability

Repair Less, Stay Stress Free: Modular Upkeep System

How does repairing and upkeep work? Our machines follow strict component modularity. Never troubleshoot circuit boards on location—simply hot-swap the block in under 3 minutes.

3-Minute Hot-Swap

Power supplies, coin mechs, displays, and logic boards use quick-release molex connectors. Anyone can replace a component without soldering.

  • Zero on-site soldering required.
  • Numbered wire harnesses prevent errors.
  • 99.4% average uptime achieved.
